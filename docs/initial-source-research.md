## Scoring policy

Do not rank providers by advertised headline earnings alone. A source should be scored only after recording:

1. Verified eligibility for the user's actual circumstances.
2. Net payout after platform, conversion, and withdrawal fees.
3. Approval/rejection rate based on the operator's own completed work.
4. Median active minutes per approved task and effective net hourly yield.
5. Minimum withdrawal and time-to-cash-out.
6. Account reliability, task inventory, and terms-review freshness.
7. Whether an official API or expressly permitted integration exists.

## Automation boundary

The research pack does not authorize automated clicks, impressions, surveys, reviews, account creation, or task submissions. EarnOrbit may automate public-source monitoring, permitted API synchronization, reminders, calculations, and ledger reconciliation. Human participation remains required wherever the provider expects genuine human judgment or interaction.

## PTC-specific research

No PTC provider is marked active in this seed list. Add a PTC provider only after its first-party terms, traffic rules, anti-bot policy, payout proof, minimum withdrawal, fee schedule, and eligibility requirements have been verified. A high advertised click rate is not evidence of positive net hourly income.
