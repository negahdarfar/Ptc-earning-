## Enforcement

- Do not add these programs as active opportunities for country IR.
- Do not recommend VPNs, false residence details, borrowed identities, or proxy accounts to bypass restrictions.
- A crypto payout option does not override an explicit country restriction.
- Preserve the source URL and review date when recording an eligibility decision.
- If a platform changes its policy, require a fresh review of the exact product/program terms before changing its status.

This document records public terms, not legal advice. Sanctions, local law, KYC, and payment-provider restrictions may independently apply.
